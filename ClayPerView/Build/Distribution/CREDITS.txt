CLAY-PER-VIEW • FAMOUS LAST BLOWS

A fictional late-night wrestling broadcast starring Rex Granite, Lola Glitz,
Chef Bruno Flambe, Chip Sterling, DJ Wubz, Duchess Winnifred, Vince Voltage
and Madame Mystica. Dirk Bluster and Nigel Quibble call the action.

The character, arena and prop meshes were built in Blender. Unreal Engine
runs the game and its stepped puppet animation. Fight effects, entrance
music, crowd beds and the score were synthesized for the production.

Original scripted dialogue is performed with the open-weight Kokoro-82M v1.0
text-to-speech model, rendered offline using kokoro-onnx. Voice presets are
Onyx (ring announcer and coach), Puck (Dirk), and George (Nigel).
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx
