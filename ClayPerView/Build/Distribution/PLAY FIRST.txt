CLAY-PER-VIEW
Famous Last Blows • macOS • 1.0.0

Open Clay-Per-View.app to go on air. The game runs offline on Apple silicon.
Extract the whole archive before launching. Sound and picture settings are saved locally.

YOUR FIRST BROADCAST

Choose Ring School for a hands-on rehearsal. Walk over to Chip, land a jab,
catch a punch on your guard, grab and body-slam him, then fire your signature.

Choose Ratings Run and cast your star. Each of the three broadcasts has a
different opponent and an optional crowd challenge. Bank up to three stars
per bout. The finalist who wins Prime Time gets the television title belt.
Each headliner keeps a personal best on each difficulty.

Exhibition lets you cast both corners. For a couch match choose Two Players.
During knockouts, get close to your reeling rival and press Signature to
trigger your star's Curtain Call. With exhibition pins enabled, slam your opponent,
then press Grab beside them with empty hands. The defender can tap Jab when
the moving needle enters gold. Three counts wins the round.

CONTROLS

Menus          Arrows or WASD • Enter to select • Esc to return • mouse to click
Solo           WASD move • J jab • K heavy • L grab or throw • I hold guard
               Space dodge • Left Shift run • U signature • O taunt • P / Esc pause
Two players    Left: WASD move, F/G/H/R strike/heavy/grab/guard, T signature
               Right: arrows move, comma/period/slash/M strike/heavy/grab/guard
               N dodge, Right Shift run, L signature, semicolon taunt
Gamepad        Stick move • X jab • Y heavy • B grab • RB guard • A dodge
               RT run • LB signature • Start pause (Xbox button names)

For a quieter broadcast, open Options > Sound Mixer. Master, music, voices,
effects, crowd and menu sounds have separate levels. Dialogue captions cover
the booth, announcer and coach in English or Brazilian Portuguese.
Under Picture Quality, try High first and watch the live FPS and frame time.
Change shadows, effects or render scale to find the balance for your Mac.
Impact camera shake and arena flash bulbs can be switched off under Options.

GUIA RÁPIDO • PORTUGUÊS (BRASIL)

Abra o aplicativo e escolha Ring School para ensaiar. No modo solo, ande com
WASD, use J para um jab, I para defender, L para agarrar e K para derrubar.
Com a barra cheia, U ativa o golpe especial. Em Ratings Run, vença três lutas
para conquistar o cinturão. Cada desafio da torcida rende uma estrela extra.
Para ativar as legendas em português, entre em Options > Sound Mixer,
deixe Dialogue subtitles em ON e mude Subtitle language para PORTUGUÊS (BR).
