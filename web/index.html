Tonight’s running order

What’s on

A wrestling brawler staged like a late-night broadcast.

Part one The modes

Ratings Run
Three broadcasts stand between your star and the television belt: the Cold Open, the Prop Department and Prime Time. Each can earn three stars: one for the win, one for finishing with at least half your health, one for the crowd’s challenge.
Ring School
The coach walks you through a jab, a block, a body slam and a signature against a live partner, then sends you to casting.
Two Players
One keyboard shared down the middle, or a gamepad each, for a couch exhibition.
Exhibition and Watch a Bout
Cast both corners and set the rules. Or let the computer play both sides and listen to the booth.

Part two The fight

Signatures and Curtain Calls
Landing hits, taking hits and taunting fill your ratings. A full bar buys your signature move. Knock a rival out in the deciding round and they are left reeling: get close, press Signature, and the Curtain Call does the rest.
The crowd throws things in
Folding chair, guitar, gold star award, frying pan, mallet, rubber chicken and a studio clapperboard. Pick one up, swing it, throw it.
Pinfalls
With pins switched on in an exhibition, cover a downed opponent. The defender taps jab in the gold zone to kick out before the referee counts three.
Instant Replay
The results screen can play back the closing seconds from the arena camera.

Part three The production

Stepped stop-motion
Every clip is a handful of hand-sculpted poses, shown at 12 frames per second by default, with replacement mouths and hands the way stop-motion animators do it.
A full house
6,700 spectators sit, stand, bob and wave as the bout heats up. Some of them brought placards.
The booth and the referee
Dirk Bluster and Nigel Quibble call the action and have opinions. The referee counts, and can be flattened by a stray shot.
Subtitles and difficulty
Dialogue subtitles in English and Brazilian Portuguese, and three levels of computer opponent.

Pause Esc or P Start
  • Menus take a click or a tap, or WS or the arrow keys with Enter to select and Esc to go back.
  • The first gamepad is player one and a second gamepad is player two. With a single pad in a two-player bout, the pad plays the left side and the arrow-key half of the keyboard plays the right.
  • On a phone or tablet the browser version adds touch controls.

House rules

  • Strikes, grabs, blocks

    Strikes beat grabs, grabs beat blocks, blocks beat strikes. Three jabs chain into a cross and a launching uppercut. Heavy plus toward your opponent is a big boot; heavy on its own is a haymaker.

  • A blocked strike recoils

    The combo stops and the attacker is open for a moment, so block, then hit back or grab. Catch a strike in the first 130 milliseconds of your guard for a clean block and extra ratings.

  • In a grab

    Jab is a headbutt, heavy is a body slam, and grab plus a direction whips them into the ropes. Mash to escape a hold.

  • The ropes are springy

    Run into them to rebound. Attacks out of a rebound hit harder: jab becomes a clothesline, heavy a dropkick.

  • On the mat

    Against a downed opponent, jab is a stomp and heavy is a flying splash. Down yourself? Tap any button to get up sooner. You can only be hit twice while you are down.

  • Ratings

    The bar under your health fills as you land hits, take hits and taunt. Full ratings buy your signature move. Taunting can also earn you something from the crowd.

  • Curtain Call

    Lose the deciding round by knockout and you are left reeling on your feet. Your rival has a few seconds to get close and press Signature for their finishing move.

  • Mirror match

    Pick the same celebrity for both corners and the one on the right turns up as a tinted stunt double.

No install!

In your browser

Press play and the bell rings. It runs in any current desktop browser with WebGL 2, with a keyboard or gamepads. On phones and tablets it adds touch controls.

Play now
  • One or two players on one keyboard
  • Gamepads supported
  • Your options and records are saved in the browser

On your Mac

The desktop original, built on Unreal Engine 5. For Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. The download is about 415 MB.

Download for Mac

Before the first launch

The Mac build is not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks for confirmation the first time. Extract the whole archive, then right-click the app and choose Open, or allow it under System Settings › Privacy & Security.

End credits

Made with

Clay by script, voices by model, bruises by hand.

Engines
The desktop game is C++ on Unreal Engine 5. The browser version is a TypeScript port on three.js, built from the same models, animation and audio.
Clay
Every model was sculpted by Blender scripts: the eight headliners, the referee, the commentators, the ring, the arena, the crowd and the props.
Voices
Original scripted dialogue is performed by the open-weight Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (Apache-2.0), rendered ahead of time with kokoro-onnx. Voice presets: Onyx for the ring announcer and coach, Puck for Dirk, George for Nigel.
Sound
Fight effects, entrance music, crowd beds and the score were synthesized for the production.
Type
Titan One, Anton, Barlow Condensed and Bangers, under the SIL Open Font License (Titan One, Anton, Barlow Condensed, Bangers).
Production disclosure
Generative AI assisted the game design, dialogue, localization, programming, the Blender scripts that make the characters and scenery, and the music and effects synthesis scripts.
Fiction
The league, the belt, the commentators and all eight headliners are invented. No real person appears in the game.
