## Entry copy

**One-line pitch**  
Eight clay celebrities have three live broadcasts to win a television title belt. Even the prop department gets a say.

**Short description**  
Pick a washed-up action star, a furious TV chef or one of six other fictional headliners. The Ratings Run books three bouts around your choice. Chain hits for the crowd, return a borrowed clapperboard the hard way and deliver a signature Curtain Call on prime time. The referee counts, two commentators keep score of the indignities, and the ringside camera saves the finish. Hand a friend the other half of the keyboard for a couch exhibition.

**Descrição em português**  
Oito celebridades de massinha disputam um cinturão em três transmissões ao vivo. Escolha sua estrela e entre no ringue. A torcida pede uma sequência de golpes, a produção empresta uma claquete por sua conta e risco e o horário nobre exige um golpe final digno de reprise. O juiz conta até três, a dupla de comentaristas narra o vexame e a câmera do ringue grava o desfecho. No modo de exibição, duas pessoas podem lutar no mesmo teclado.

## A first play for the jury

1. Open the extracted app. Choose **Ring School** and complete the four coached moves.
2. Choose **Ratings Run**. Cast **Lola Glitz** for a three-bout card against Madame Mystica, DJ Wubz and Duchess Winnifred. Land the crowd challenge in each episode to earn an extra star. A win and finishing with at least half health earn the other two.
3. In the deciding round, close the distance when the opponent is dazed and tap **U** to trigger Mic Drop. After a win, **Instant Replay** plays the ending from the arena camera.
4. For two players, choose **Two Players**, pick both stars and share a keyboard or use controllers. Slam a rival and press Grab with empty hands to try for a three-count. The defender taps Jab in the gold zone to lift a shoulder.

The performance meter is under **Options → Picture Quality**. In the **Sound Mixer**, the language setting changes dialogue subtitles. In general Options, arena flash bulbs and impact camera shake can be disabled independently. The game stores scores and settings locally.

The playtest report includes full-health match counts, the four measured 1080p presets and a Low/Ultra frame comparison. [Playtest report](PLAYTEST.md)

## Production disclosure for the form

Generative AI assisted game design, dialogue, localization, programming, the Blender scripts used to make characters and scenery, and the music and effects synthesis scripts. Kokoro-82M v1.0 synthesized the original English dialogue for the game and trailer. Speech is rendered ahead of time and plays offline. The ring, cast, title belt and broadcast identity were created for Clay-Per-View. The trailer combines game footage with the game's sculpted assets, original music and additional scripted commentary. Credits identify the model, voice presets, engine, typefaces and authoring tools. [Production credits](../ClayPerView/Build/Distribution/CREDITS.txt)
